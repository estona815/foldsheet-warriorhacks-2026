Download a two-sided calibration sheet, print at actual size on your chosen paper, and inspect the TOP labels. Adjust your printer’s duplex setting or the back-side rotation before a full run.

FoldSheet lays out pages. It cannot detect your printer’s feed direction, binding behavior or printable area. Thick booklets may need smaller signatures or professional finishing.
